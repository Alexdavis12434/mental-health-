Top 5 Mental Health Tips

  • Follow the 50/10 Rule: 50 min study, 10 min break
  • Prioritize Sleep: 7–9 hours is not optional
  • Eat Brain Fuel: Healthy snacks + hydration
  • Move: Take a walk, stretch, dance
  • Talk Kindly to Yourself: Self-compassion is powerful
